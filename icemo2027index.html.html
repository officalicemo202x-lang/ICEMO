Queries & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a Class 9th student eligible for I.CEMO 2027?

Yes! Exceptionally gifted students from Class 8 and Class 9 are fully eligible to apply and attempt the olympiad, although the examination syllabus benchmarks Class 10th, 11th, and 12th standards.

How does I.CEMO compare with IOQM / INMO level toughness?

I.CEMO is calibrated at an Extreme Tough level. It combines non-routine IOQM combinatorial & number theory depth with advanced multi-numerical and matrix-match structural rigor.

What does the 'I' in I.CEMO stand for?

Please note: 'I' in I.CEMO does NOT mean International. It denotes the specialized Institute/Institutional framework of the Common Mathematics Entrance Olympiad.

What is the qualifying cutoff percentage?

The minimum nominal qualifying cutoff is strictly set at 47% of Nominal Marks (~72.85 Marks out of 155 Marks).

What happens if my camera or mic gets disconnected?

Continuous proctor logging requires an active camera and mic stream. Prolonged interruptions may lead to immediate automated flag submission and disqualification.
